About Me

I am a PhD student in the Tri-Institute Program in Computational Biology at Weill Cornell Medicine, working in the Greenbaum Lab on epigenetics of retro-transposable elements in cancer. With a strong background in genomics and computational biology, I focus on understanding the molecular mechanisms underlying cancer development.

My research expertise spans circulating tumor DNA analysis, mutational signatures, copy number analysis, and whole genome sequencing. I have extensive experience in cloud computing, NGS data analysis, and developing computational pipelines for cancer genomics research.

I am passionate about advancing cancer research through computational approaches and have contributed to understanding breast cancer genomics in African populations, particularly focusing on non-invasive detection methods and genetic ancestry analysis.
